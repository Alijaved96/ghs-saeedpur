Admission Checklist

Required Documents

Please bring clear photocopies along with originals where verification is required by the school office.

01

Student B-Form / Birth Certificate

NADRA Child Registration Certificate (B-Form/CRC) or birth certificate, as applicable.

02

Parent / Guardian CNIC

Copy of the valid CNIC of the student’s parent or legal guardian.

03

Passport-Size Photographs

Two recent passport-size photographs of the student.

04

Previous Result / Progress Card

Latest result card or progress report for students who have attended another school.

05

School Leaving Certificate

Original school leaving / transfer certificate for transfer cases or admission to higher classes, where applicable.

06

Other Supporting Documents

Domicile/PRC or any additional document may be requested if required for a particular admission category.

Note: Documentary requirements may vary by academic session and class. Final requirements will be confirmed by the school administration.
