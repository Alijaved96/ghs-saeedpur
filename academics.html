Academics

A clear pathway from early learning through secondary education, with attention to knowledge, skills, discipline and student growth.

Learning Pathway

ECE/KG to Class X

Our academic programme supports learners across early years, primary, middle and secondary stages.

🧩

Early Years

ECE/KG learning focused on foundations, participation, language development and positive classroom habits.

✏️

Primary

Core literacy, numeracy, general knowledge and structured classroom learning.

🔬

Middle

Broader subject learning with increasing emphasis on understanding, application and independent study.

🎓

Secondary

Focused preparation for Class IX and X learning, examinations and the next stage of education.

Academic Culture

Learning beyond memorisation.

The school aims to encourage regular attendance, classroom participation, disciplined study habits and a respectful learning culture. Academic activities are supported by assessment, teacher guidance and student involvement.

  • Structured classroom learning
  • Regular academic assessment
  • Encouragement of reading, writing and problem-solving
  • Student participation in school activities
  • Preparation for secondary-level examinations
